ABOUT UNITED DIESEL CARE

Keeping Diesel Systems Running Right.

United Diesel Care is a specialist diesel workshop serving customers in and around Nagercoil. The workshop focuses on diesel fuel-system service, CRDI injector testing and repair, and diagnostic support.
